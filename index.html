About VK Mobile Cabinet Movers

VK Mobile Cabinet Movers Sdn Bhd specialises in professional mobile mechanical and manual cabinet moving services.

We provide dismantling, shifting and fixing services for office cabinets, library shelving systems and heavy equipment.

Our services also include cargo lorry transport, lorry crane, self-loading crane, warehousing, warehouse container offloading and logistics support.

We handle heavy and sensitive equipment with careful planning, proper handling procedures and professional moving techniques.
